Needlepoint Roman 1.000
A hybrid text for humans, hybrids, and bots, by Matt Schober (iamtheneedle.com).

Every letter A-Z / a-z carries a five-dot binary rail beneath it that encodes
its position in the alphabet (A = 1 ... Z = 26). Filled dot = 1, ring = 0.
The word checksum seals and line intent bars shown on iamtheneedle.com are
drawn by the website, not by the font.

Styles: Regular, Bold, Italic, Bold Italic.
  .ttf   - install on Mac, Windows, or Linux (double-click, then Install)
  .woff2 - for websites (@font-face)
