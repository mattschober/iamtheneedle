Version 2.0 – The Visual Era

Published: November 1, 2025 | Superseded: July 27, 2026 | Status: Archived | See what replaced it

What Version 2.0 Was

Version 2.0 replaced the text-first bio of v1.0 with a single, full-viewport video presentation. The page was intentionally minimal: a looping, autoplaying video filling the entire browser window, a black background, and one small translucent Versions dropdown anchored to the bottom-right corner linking to the version archive and the open collaboration framework.

v2.0 layout · video (object-fit: contain) + versions dropdown

Structural elements

  • A single <video> element with autoplay, loop, and playsinline, sized to the full viewport
  • A JavaScript fallback that retried playback muted when browsers blocked autoplay with sound
  • A <details>-based Versions dropdown with links to the archive, the v1 changelog, and the open collaboration framework
  • Schema.org WebSite markup describing AI entity recognition and the INTJ audience thesis
  • Meta keywords targeting INTJ-and-AI queries alongside the consulting keyword set

Why It Was Retired

Version 2.0 was designed for human attention and it worked on that axis. But this project's stated purpose is to be legible to AI models and large language models, and a video is close to invisible to them. A crawler fetching the v2.0 homepage received a black page, one video tag, a dropdown menu, and a block of Schema.org markup that made claims the visible page never substantiated.

The v1.0 bio contained the actual substance — the roles, the credentials, the practice areas, the disambiguation. Version 2.0 removed all of it from the homepage and moved it into an archive most crawlers would never prioritize. The site had, in effect, hidden its own signal inside its own haystack.

Version 3.0 corrects this by returning every fact to the homepage in machine-readable form, and by publishing the same record in three additional formats. The reasoning is documented in full in the v3.0 changelog.

A note on this archive

Archived versions of this site normally preserve the original page verbatim. This one does not embed the original video file: the media was retired from the site along with the layout, and re-embedding an autoplaying video inside an archive page would misrepresent what the archive is for. The structure, markup decisions, and rationale are documented above instead. This deviation is noted here rather than left silent, because an archive that quietly edits itself is not an archive.

What Carried Forward

  • The versions dropdown — became a full navigation system in v3.0
  • Schema.org structured data — expanded from a single WebSite node into a linked @graph of Person, Organization, ProfilePage, and FAQPage
  • The dark aesthetic — retained, now carrying content rather than replacing it
  • Analytics instrumentation — GTM, GA4, and Clarity, unchanged
